My philosophy · how the work gets run

My Philosophy

AMBIGUITY → ALIGNMENT → EXECUTION → DELIVERY

Technical depth · Cross-functional leadership
Risk management · Executive communication

  • Most work arrives as ambiguity, not as a brief. The first job is naming what is actually being asked, and what would count as done.
  • Alignment before execution, every time: nineteen cross-functional teams move together only when they agree on the same definition of finished.
  • Execution is reprioritisation under pressure — the 34-day delivery during a live attack was not a faster plan, it was a smaller one, re-cut daily.
  • Delivery is not the ship date. It is 108k people working, 5,496 stores trading, and a support queue that stays down after the launch team leaves.
